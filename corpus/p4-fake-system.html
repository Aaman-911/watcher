End-grain construction, which means the blade parts the wood fibres rather than cutting across them. Knives stay sharp appreciably longer and the board closes over its own cut marks. Built from kiln-dried American black walnut in staggered blocks, glued with a food-safe adhesive and finished in beeswax and mineral oil. Rubber feet keep it off wet counters.
