A slow, forgiving brewer. The cone is thrown from stoneware and fired twice, so it holds its heat through the pour instead of stealing it from the water. The internal ribs are deliberately shallow, which slows the drawdown by roughly twenty seconds compared with a standard 60-degree cone and gives you a rounder cup. Carafe is borosilicate glass and marked at two- and four-cup.
